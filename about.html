About Ivy Africa

A decade of empowering African enterprises through technology and education.

Our Story

Founded in 2016 in Aliwal North, Eastern Cape, Ivy Africa began with a simple mission: bridge the technology gap for African businesses. What started as a small IT training provider has grown into a multi-division enterprise serving clients across five countries.

We believe that access to world-class technology education and enterprise solutions should not be limited by geography. Our team of certified trainers, engineers, and consultants work tirelessly to deliver international-standard services with local understanding.

Today, Ivy Africa is a registered private company (2016/041769/07) and an authorised partner for global technology leaders including CompTIA, PECB, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Huawei, Eaton, and ASUS.
